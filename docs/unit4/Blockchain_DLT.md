# Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology

A distributed ledger is a shared record replicated across nodes. Blockchain groups records into linked blocks. Nodes use a consensus method to agree on valid updates, and cryptographic links make changes to earlier blocks evident.

Applications include cryptocurrency, supply-chain provenance and shared audit records. Advantages are transparency and reduced dependence on one owner. Limitations include throughput, storage growth, governance and possible energy cost depending on consensus.

Viva: **What is consensus?** A way for distributed nodes to agree on valid records. **What is immutability?** Earlier records are difficult to change without detection.
